Community Fish Detector weights are not bundled in this project.

Download from:
https://github.com/filippovarini/community-fish-detector/releases

Recommended first model for volunteer laptops:
- RF-DETR Nano 640 .pth

After download, place it here:
weights/cfd-2026.02.02-rf-detr-nano-640.pth

Then update configs/cfd_counter.yaml:
model:
  weights: weights/<your_downloaded_file>.pth
  resolution: 640

For RF-DETR Small/Medium, use resolution: 1024.
